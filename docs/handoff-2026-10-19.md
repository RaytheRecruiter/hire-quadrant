## N19 QA pass — Employer Company Dashboard
All of the following were confirmed live and broken, then fixed:
- **Account deletion**: `delete_my_data()` crashed on its first statement (wrong column — `company_reviews.user_id` doesn't exist, it's `author_id`) and even when fixed, never actually deleted the `auth.users` row — just data cleanup, login still worked after "deletion."
- **Employer resume access**: storage RLS gap, employers could never download an applicant's resume (`createSignedUrl` → "Object not found" even though the file existed).
- **Applications visibility**: the original RLS policy matched `job_applications.source_company` against the company name, but the apply form never populates that column — every employer saw "No Applicants Yet" regardless of real applications on file. Replaced with a relational check through `jobs.company_id` → `company_members`.
- **Analytics tab universal "forbidden"**: `get_company_dashboard_stats` checked a `company_admins` table created 2026-04-23 that has had zero rows ever inserted into it — superseded by `company_members` (2026-04-29) and never updated. This table turned out to gate **6 more things**, all fixed in the same pass: Reviews, Review Responses, Page Views, Why Join Us content blocks, Q&A answering, Company Updates.
- **2 cross-tenant data leaks**: `recruiter_activity` and `employer_benchmarks` RPCs are `SECURITY DEFINER` (bypasses RLS) with no check that the caller belongs to the company being queried — any logged-in user could pull another company's team activity or benchmark stats.
- **Unlock-credit system**: same missing-caller-check pattern (`unlocks_remaining`), plus a legacy-account gap in `unlock_candidate` (companies claimed after the one-time `company_members` backfill had no row there at all).
- **Root cause found**: `claim_company()` never created a `company_members` row, only set `user_profiles.company_id` directly — meaning every company claimed since 2026-04-29 was invisible to most of the dashboard, including a hard lockout on the Team Members page. Fixed at the source, with a backfill for every account already stuck.

## Three follow-on area audits (beyond the original scope)
- **Employer job-posting flow** (`NewJobModal.tsx` / `CompanyJobsList.tsx`): found the `jobs` table's INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE RLS only ever checked `user_profiles`, with zero awareness of `company_members` — meaning the entire permission system (Standard users without `post_jobs`/`edit_jobs`, or a *deactivated* team member) was UI-only, not enforced in the database. A fired recruiter's still-valid session could keep posting/editing/deleting jobs. Fixed.
- **Stripe webhook handler**: found zero idempotency protection anywhere — no tracking of processed Stripe event IDs, no unique constraint on `stripe_checkout_session_id`. A retried webhook (Stripe's documented, expected behavior on timeout/non-2xx) would double-credit purchased contact credits and create duplicate sponsorship-order rows. Fixed with a `stripe_webhook_events` table + early-exit on duplicate delivery. **Already deployed** (`supabase functions deploy stripe-webhook`) — doesn't wait on a frontend build.
- **Resume-parsing pipeline**: found `years_experience` was the one field that didn't follow the feature's own stated "don't overwrite manually-set values" design — every resume re-upload silently clobbered a candidate's manually-entered years of experience (confirmed settable via `Onboarding.tsx`). Fixed with a null-check (not a truthy check, since "0 years" is a legitimate manual value).
