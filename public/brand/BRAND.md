# Nexegin brand

## Promise
Find your next IT role.

## Brand statement
Nexegin is the job board for IT and technical professionals. Explore IT jobs, save opportunities, and manage your applications in one place. As our career data grows, we show the routes real engineers took between roles, so candidates see what to learn next and employers see who can actually do the job.

## Positioning (internal)
For IT and technical candidates and the employers hiring them, Nexegin is the job board that is narrow on purpose — IT and technical roles only — instead of a general board where technical postings get lost in the noise.

## Taglines
- Primary: Find your next IT role.
- Supporting: Explore IT jobs, save opportunities, and manage your applications in one place.
- Secondary: Tech hiring, mapped. (use once the career-path data is live)
- Employer-facing: Hire from the paths that actually lead to the job.

## Voice
Direct, practical, no jargon. Say what happens and when. Never promise what the product does not yet enforce — no response, feedback, screening, acknowledgment, or placement guarantees of any kind.

## Claims that must be true before launch
1. "Career paths": only show transitions seen by 5+ people; label AI-generated paths as AI-generated.

## Name
nexegin (lowercase wordmark; "Nexegin" in running text). Pronunciation: pick one and publish it on the About page.

## Mark
A rising stepped path (career moves) ending in a signal node (the answer / the offer). Not a letterform; scales down to 16px.
